The shop

We cut hair for the whole neighbourhood.

Imperial Barbers is on Great South Road, in among the shops you already drive past. Some people come in every second Saturday and sit in the same chair. Others spot a free chair through the window and come in on the spot. Both work for us.

We do skin fades and scissor work, beards, colour, hot towel shaves and the full sit-down groom. Whatever you are after, we will talk it through first—what you want, what will suit you, and what will still look right in three weeks when you are back at work.

Why locals choose us

Five reasons people keep coming back.

  • Walk in whenever

    No appointment needed. If a chair is free it is yours, and if it is not, the wait is usually short—with somewhere decent to sit while you wait.

  • A proper welcome

    You will get a conversation if you want one and quiet if you would rather not talk. Either way nobody is hurrying you back out the door.

  • There is a pool table

    A full-size table sits by the window. Waiting your turn becomes a frame or two, which is also the easiest way to keep the kids happy.

  • Room to sit back

    A wide floor, a full row of chairs and the air conditioning on. Nobody is working on top of anybody, and you are not squeezed into a corner.

  • Every age, every hair type

    First haircuts and grey trims in the same afternoon. Curly, straight, thick or fine—it all gets cut here.

Inside

Built to be sat in.

The floor is wide and the ceiling is high, so the shop never feels like a queue. There is a bench by the door if someone came with you, screens on the wall, and the air conditioning going through summer.

It is a modern room with an old-fashioned habit: you are welcome to stay a bit longer than the haircut takes. Plenty of people do.
